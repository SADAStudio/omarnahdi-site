Privacy Policy — Toybox Knights

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Toybox Knights is a game by SADA. It is made for families and played without an account. This page says what information the game handles, who sees it, and what you can do about it.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. The game has no sign-up, no login, no chat, no friends list and no server of ours. We never ask for a name, an email address, a birthday or a location, and we never see payment details.

What stays on your device and in your own iCloud

Your progress (nights finished, stars, rescued toys, coins, cards, Armory levels, looks, trophies, settings) is saved on the device. On iPhone and iPad it is also saved in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage) so it can follow you to a new device. We cannot read it: it lives in your Apple account, under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the game from all your devices and turning off iCloud for it removes it.

Game Center

If your device is signed in to Apple's Game Center, trophies and two leaderboards (Arena waves, fastest Night 5) are sent to Game Center. This is optional and run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the game works the same without it.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Apple handles the payment; we receive no card or account details. A grown-up question (hold a button for three seconds, then type the answer to a question written in number words, or a times sum) comes before every purchase, before Restore Purchases and before any link out of the game. Parents can stop purchases altogether with Apple's Ask to Buy or Screen Time. Colours, Auto double and Max a toy stay with your Apple ID and come back with "Restore Purchases" in the shop; coin packs are spent in the game, so they don't come back. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Rewarded videos (advertising)

The game shows a short video advertisement only when you choose to tap "Watch to double" on a results screen, to double a reward you have already earned. There are no banner ads, no pop-up ads and no ads during play, and nothing in the game requires watching one. At most ten videos a day, with a break between two.

The videos are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) with the Unity Ads network, both run by Unity Technologies. We configure them in child-directed, non-personalized mode: ads are chosen for children and are not based on a profile of you. The game never asks for permission to track you and never uses the advertising identifier (IDFA).

To show and count the ads, Unity's software may process this information on Unity's servers:

  • a device identifier (the vendor identifier) and an anonymous id of its own, to limit how often an ad is shown and to detect fraud;
  • information about the ads shown (which ad, whether it was watched);
  • technical information about the device (model, language, screen size, connection type, performance of the ad software);
  • the country of the device, worked out from the network address;
  • purchase events, as reported by the ad software.

This information is used to show ads, to measure that they worked and to keep the ad software reliable. It is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it is not sold. Unity's own privacy policy explains how it handles it, how long it keeps it and how to ask for its deletion: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy (for ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

Children

Toybox Knights is rated 9+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The ad partner is told that the app is directed to children, which switches off personalized advertising. If you believe a child has given us personal information in any way, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • No ads: simply never tap "Watch to double"; the free reward is always paid. "Auto double" in the shop doubles rewards with no videos at all.
  • Purchases off: Apple's Ask to Buy or Screen Time (Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases).
  • Delete your data: delete the game (and its iCloud data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage). Information held by Unity can be requested or deleted through Unity's privacy page above.

Changes

If the game changes what it does with information, this page changes with it and the date above moves.
